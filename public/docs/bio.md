Matteo Migliarini is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Sapienza University of Rome, supervised by Fabio Galasso and Indro Spinelli. He works on AI safety: self-preservation in language models, generative unlearning, and mechanistic interpretability.

He is currently a fellow at LASR Labs in London, investigating scalable oversight and how to evaluate model capability once benchmarks saturate. Before that he took part in ARENA 5.0, mentored a research team at Algoverse AI, and worked as a software engineer at Accenture.

Born in Rome in 2000, he holds a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Data Science from Sapienza. He contributes to open source, mainly Inspect AI.
