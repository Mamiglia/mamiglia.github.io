Matteo Migliarini is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Sapienza University of Rome, supervised by Fabio Galasso and Indro Spinelli, with a focus on AI safety: safety measures for advanced AI systems, robust AI, and responsible deployment. He has published work on self-preservation in large language models (EMNLP'26), generative unlearning for video and human-motion models (ICLR'26, AAAI'26), multi-party human-LLM interactions on social media (@GrokSet), and mechanistic interpretability. He is currently a fellow at LASR Labs in London, investigating scalable oversight and how to evaluate model capability once benchmarks saturate, mentored by Magda Dubois (UK-AISI) and Justin Olive (Generality Labs). Previously he took part in ARENA 5.0, where his capstone on a self-suppressing attention head in GPT-2 received an honorable mention, mentored a research team at Algoverse AI, and worked as a software engineer at Accenture. Born in Rome in 2000, he holds a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Data Science from Sapienza, and contributes to open source, with substantial work on Inspect AI and occasional contributions to PyTorch.
