Matteo Migliarini is a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence and a junior researcher at ItalAI, specializing in AI safety and mechanistic interpretability. Born in Rome in 2000, he has a strong technical background in software engineering, deep learning, and computational theory. With a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering and a Master's in Data Science, Matteo has conducted research at the intersection of AI safety and interpretability. Recently he also participated in ARENA. He is passionate about understanding the internal workings of AI models and advancing safe and transparent AI systems.
